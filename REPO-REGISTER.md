A handful of product lines account for most of the account, usually with older versions left in place rather than archived.

**instructSite** - five repos carry the name: `instructsite` looks like the live one, with `InstructSiteV1.1`, `instructsitev1` and `instructesitev1` behind it as earlier or duplicated versions.

**instructBrain** - `instructbrain` is current, with `instructBrain.v1.1` and `instructBrain.v1` as the earlier Oracle builds, and `instructReport` generating reports on top of it.

**instruct tooling** - `instructsnag` (construction snagging) and `instructsurvey` (surveys) are separate products sharing the instruct branding.

**Elle Inventory** - `elleinv` is the current public build, with `elleinventories` and `Elleis` as earlier private ones.

**site-mind-forge** - `site-mind-forge` is the private original and `site-mind-forge-d4346a19` the public template generated from it.

Standalone projects: `dal-personal-agent`, `CCL`, `city-car-parks-saas`, `DIYDAY` and `your-github-guide`.
